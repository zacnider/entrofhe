## 📋 Practice Tips

### 1. Break Down Difficult Words

**Practice these words separately:**
- "comprehensive" → Say: "com-pre-hen-sive" (4 parts)
- "automation" → Say: "auto-ma-tion" (3 parts)
- "documentation" → Say: "doc-u-men-ta-tion" (5 parts)
- "functionality" → Say: "func-tion-al-i-ty" (5 parts)

### 2. Slow Down on Technical Terms

- **FHEVM** → Say each letter: "F-H-E-V-M"
- **EntropyOracle** → "EN-tro-py OR-a-cle"
- **EntropyNFT** → "EN-tro-py N-F-T"
- **IPFS** → "I-P-F-S"

### 3. Pause Points

Add natural pauses:
- After "Hi, I'm presenting EntroFHE" → [pause 1 second]
- After "Let me show you what we've built" → [pause 1 second]
- After each example name → [pause 0.5 seconds]

### 4. Emphasis Words

Stress these words (say them louder):
- **22 examples**
- **EntropyOracle**
- **automation tools**
- **Zama FHEVM**
